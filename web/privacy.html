Privacy Policy

Pregnancy Companion • Last updated: 16 August 2026

Pregnancy Companion ("the app", "we") helps expecting mothers and their families track pregnancy. We care about your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with email, Google, phone, or as a guest, we collect your email address, display name, and/or phone number to create and secure your account (via Google Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile & pregnancy details — information you enter, such as your name, age, height, weight, blood group, any health conditions you choose to add, last-period/due dates, and risk category.
  • Health tracking data — entries you log: medicines, appointments, symptoms, water, weight, blood pressure, blood sugar, sleep, baby kicks, contractions, moods, journal notes, and checklists.
  • Community content — posts and comments you choose to share in the community feed are visible to other signed-in users.
  • Notifications — a device push token to deliver reminders and alerts.

Information that stays on your device

The following never leaves your phone and is not uploaded to our servers:

  • Photos and videos you add to Memories, and your profile avatar.
  • Original prescription and lab-report images/PDFs you scan — the original file is stored only on your device.

How your data is stored

Your account and tracking data are stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) under security rules that restrict access to you and the family members or doctor you explicitly share a pregnancy with. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

AI features

When you use AI features (the "Ask Mira" assistant, symptom guidance, meal and wellness plans, prescription/lab reading, myth explanations, baby-name ideas), the relevant text — and, for scanning, the image you choose — is sent over an encrypted connection to our processing service (a Cloudflare Worker) and on to an AI model provider (Google Gemini, with a Cloudflare Workers AI fallback) to generate a response. This content is used only to produce your result and is not stored by us. Optionally, you may provide your own AI provider key, which is stored securely on your device and used only to make your own requests. AI output is educational only and is not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical advice.

How we use information

  • To provide and personalise the app's tracking, reminders and content.
  • To enable sharing with family members or a doctor you choose.
  • To deliver notifications you have enabled.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Sharing

Data is shared only: (a) with people you invite to your pregnancy (partner, family, or a doctor), limited to the categories you choose; (b) with the infrastructure providers that run the app (Google Firebase, Cloudflare, and the AI model provider) strictly to operate the service; and (c) if required by law.

Your choices & rights

  • You can edit your profile and pregnancy details in the app at any time.
  • You can remove community posts you created.
  • You can delete your account from the app (Profile → Delete account), which removes your account and associated data.
  • You can turn notifications on or off in your device settings.

Children

The app is intended for adults (expecting mothers and their families) and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
